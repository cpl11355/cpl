理念之一 · 证据先行CREED 01 · EVIDENCE

证据先行Evidence First

先验证问题是否成立，再讨论方案是否漂亮。Confirm the question is real before polishing the answer.

原则RULE区分事实、推断与待验证假设。Separate facts, inferences and open assumptions.
动作ACTION记录来源、核对口径、标注数据边界。Log sources, align definitions, flag boundaries.
结果RESULT让结论能被复核，而非只能被相信。Make conclusions reviewable, not merely believable.
理念之二 · 花钱有纪律CREED 02 · DISCIPLINE

花钱有纪律Capital Discipline

每一次投入，都要对应一项待验证的关键假设。Every spend should test a material assumption.

原则RULE先小步验证，不以规模掩盖不确定性。Validate in small steps; never hide uncertainty with scale.
动作ACTION设预算上限、阶段门与下一笔资金条件。Set budget caps, stage gates and release conditions.
结果RESULT在可控成本内获得继续或停止的依据。Earn evidence to advance or stop at controlled cost.
理念之三 · 结果可验证CREED 03 · OUTCOMES

结果可验证Measurable Outcomes

交付不是“报告完成”，而是客户可以据此行动。Delivery means a client can act, not merely receive a report.

原则RULE把建议落到负责人、时间与判断标准。Anchor advice to owners, timing and decision criteria.
动作ACTION给出优先级、行动项、风险提示与复盘节点。Provide priorities, actions, risks and review points.
结果RESULT将复杂信息转化为可执行的下一步。Turn complex information into executable next steps.
