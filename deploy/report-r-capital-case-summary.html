一、机构定位Positioning

涉案机构（代号 R·Capital）为纽约州注册商业公司，2025 年新设：非银行投融资机构（NBFI），面向个人消费贷款、小微周转与设备及应收账款贷款、短期过桥与小额商业项目贷款，外加投资者资金管理与放贷撮合。特征：快速审批、灵活结构、小到中型金额，覆盖五大区中小客户群。

The subject institution (codename R·Capital), a NY business corporation formed in 2025: a non-bank finance institution serving consumer installments, small-business working-capital, equipment and receivables loans, short bridge facilities and small commercial projects, plus investor-fund administration and loan matching. Fast approval, flexible structures, small-to-mid tickets across the five boroughs.

二、行业大周期Industry Cycle

美国私募信贷 2025 年市场规模超 1.7 万亿美元（公开市场口径），非银行机构已成信贷体系第二支柱。2023–2024 年区域银行危机后传统银行惜贷、审批趋严，叠加纽约区位与产业基础，恰是区域非银行机构的窗口期。

US private credit exceeds $1.7T in 2025 (public-market reading); non-bank lenders are now the system's second pillar. After the 2023–2024 regional-bank stress, traditional banks lend grudgingly with tighter screens — a window for regional non-bank players, anchored by New York's location and industry base.

三、目标市场Target Market

个人端消费信贷系统性回升，小微端存在结构性融资缺口，高利率周期重塑期限与定价：典型期限 1–10 年。客群划分、贷款场景与需求结构决定资金投向优先级，而非撒网式投放。

Consumer credit is cyclically recovering while small firms face a structural funding gap; the high-rate cycle reshapes tenors and pricing around a typical 1–10 year structure. Segmentation, use cases and demand structure set capital-allocation priority — no spray-and-pray deployment.

四、风控与合规Risk & Compliance

  • 全链路风控：获客、审批、放款、回款、催收
  • 三类风险分别盯防：流动性风险（回款延迟、投放中断、极端折价转让）、操作风险、合规与声誉风险
  • 纽约州 NYDFS 监管长期趋严是硬约束，风控稳、合规严是生存线
  • End-to-end risk control across sourcing, approval, disbursement, collection and recovery
  • Three books watched separately: liquidity, operational, and compliance plus reputational risk
  • A structurally tightening NYDFS regime is the hard constraint

五、双轨判断与条件Dual-Track Call

结论：处行业黄金赛道与需求高度集中的区位，做“银行 + 非银行”双轨机会判断。若能持续做到风控稳、合规严、资金稳，则具备成为纽约区域头部非银行贷款平台的现实基础，中长期具资产管理平台化与资本市场化潜力；任一条件不满足即触发 Go / No-Go 否决。

Verdict: a golden-track industry in a demand-dense location, assessed on a bank-plus-non-bank dual track. Sustained discipline on risk, compliance and funding is the qualifying condition for becoming a leading regional non-bank lending platform, with longer-term asset-management and capital-markets optionality; missing any condition trips the Go/No-Go veto.

六、产品与期限体系Product & Tenor System

三大板块分工：个人消费贷款高频小额快周转，企业经营贷款中额稳定为主收益，资本撮合与联合放贷轻资产高利润率，设计遵循标准化、可复制、可分层、可打包。

Three blocks with distinct roles: consumer loans (high-frequency, small-ticket, fast turnover), SME loans (mid-ticket, stable, core earnings), and capital matching with co-lending (asset-light, high-margin), designed to be standardized, replicable, tiered and packageable.

个人单笔 $3,000–$30,000，期限 6/12/24/36 个月，固定 APR 按风险分层定价，审批看工资流水、入账记录、雇佣稳定与还款历史；小微单笔 $50,000–$500,000，6–12 个月周转或 12–24 个月经营。

Consumer tickets run $3,000–$30,000 in 6/12/24/36-month tenors with fixed-APR risk-tiered pricing, underwritten on payroll flows, bank credits, job stability and repayment history; SME tickets run $50,000–$500,000 for 6–12 month working capital or 12–24 month mid-term needs.

设备与项目融资单笔 $50,000–$1,000,000，分期支付并以设备或项目收益权质押，关键看设备二级变现能力与项目持续现金流；联合放贷中输出客源、风控与贷后管理，以上为模型咨询结论。

Equipment and project facilities run $50,000–$1,000,000 in installments secured by equipment or project revenue rights, keyed to secondary-market resale value and sustained cash flow; in co-lending the platform contributes sourcing, risk control and post-loan management — all model-based advisory conclusions.

七、风控框架Risk Framework

信用风险是最核心的基础风险：个人端源于收入不稳、多头借贷与短期就业中断，企业端源于行业竞争、成本不稳与经营波动，现金流模型仍面临数据造假与异常扰动。

Credit risk is the core underlying risk: retail exposure stems from unstable income, stacked borrowing and short job interruptions, SME exposure from competition, cost instability and cycle swings, while cash-flow models still face falsified data and abnormal spikes.

集中度风险不容忽视：资产或集中于餐饮、零售、物流、电商，叠加单一区域与商圈依赖，景气同步下行即引发集中违约；高利率同时推高还款压力并压缩利差。

Concentration risk is material: assets may cluster in food service, retail, logistics and e-commerce with single-area or single-district dependence, so a synchronized downturn triggers clustered defaults, while high rates lift repayment stress and squeeze spreads.

  • 多层级风控模型与多行业、多区域分散
  • 单一客户授信与单一行业集中度上限
  • 多元资金来源与备用流动性通道
  • 合规手册、操作流程与内控制度标准化
  • Tiered risk models with multi-industry, multi-area diversification
  • Single-borrower caps and single-industry concentration limits
  • Diversified funding sources with standby liquidity lines
  • Standardized compliance manual, operating procedures and internal controls

八、组织与成本结构Organization & Cost Structure

收入来自利息、手续费、管理费与撮合服务费；成本由资金成本、坏账准备、人力成本、系统与合规成本构成；获客经商会、会计师事务所、POS 服务商与地产经纪，流程覆盖接洽到逾期处理。

Revenue comes from interest, fees, management and matching charges; costs comprise funding costs, loss provisions, staffing, and systems plus compliance; sourcing runs through chambers, accountants, POS providers and brokers, with workflow from first contact to delinquency handling.

组织建设聚焦风控、合规与资金管理三支团队，配套利率披露、收费透明、合同审查、AML/KYC 与内部审计；产品与风控模型可复制，远期可向新泽西、康州等周边扩展。

Organization-building centers on risk, compliance and treasury teams, backed by rate disclosure, fee transparency, contract review, AML/KYC and internal audit; replicable products and models allow later expansion into nearby New Jersey and Connecticut.
