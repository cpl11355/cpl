# CPL Immersive Brand Story Design

## Purpose

Transform the existing informational presentation into a restrained, high-impact brand story. The page must feel credible before it feels futuristic: CPL is building its platform and must not imply a launched product, an established research team, clinical progress, or partnerships that do not exist.

## Experience Direction

The page uses a two-stage visual language.

1. The opening remains light, precise, and calm. It establishes trust through generous space, clear hierarchy, and direct language.
2. The core-capabilities section moves into a deep, immersive technology environment. Scrolling advances a short sequence of full-screen scenes rather than exposing a conventional grid of cards.

The result borrows the discipline of premium technology product storytelling without reproducing any other company's visual identity.

## Story Sequence

### Entry

As the visitor reaches Core Capabilities, the page transitions from the light site surface into a navy-black environment. Navigation stays thin and translucent so that it remains available without competing with the story.

### Scene One: Value

An oversized statement fills the screen: “先算清价值，再构建证据。” The statement is the single purpose of the first scene. A quiet background system of micro-texture, measured light, and data marks provides depth without becoming decorative noise.

### Scene Two: Evidence

The statement compresses into a persistent narrative anchor. A second scene introduces the evidence principle: decisions require a credible path from value hypothesis to evidence strategy. The visual focus moves from broad atmosphere toward a precise light core.

### Scene Three: Capability

Three layers appear in sequence around the light core: value assessment, evidence strategy, and execution design. They describe a system that CPL is building, not a list of completed achievements. The visitor can continue scrolling naturally; no interaction is required to understand the order.

### Scene Four: Future

The light core extends into a forward trajectory and hands the visitor into Future Direction. The future section states exploration and platform-building clearly. It uses language such as “探索中” and “正在构建”, never language that represents future work as current fact.

### Detail Layer

After the story sequence, concise details remain available in a compact, readable format. This supports prospective partners who want fast factual orientation, services, and a clear contact route.

## Motion Rules

- Motion follows scroll position and preserves the browser's normal vertical scroll behavior.
- Full-screen scenes may briefly remain in view while their content advances, but never trap the visitor or create nested vertical scrolling.
- Each scene uses only focus, depth shift, and soft reveal. There are no bounces, rotation flourishes, rainbow effects, or uncontrolled particle fields.
- Reduced-motion preferences replace staged movement with immediate, readable transitions.
- On small screens, the experience becomes continuous sequential content rather than forcing desktop-style pinned scenes.

## Visual System

- Light phase: warm near-white surfaces, black typography, generous spacing, and exact alignment.
- Immersive phase: deep blue-black base, pale cool-white type, one controlled electric-blue light accent, low-contrast micro-texture, and a subtle grid or data field.
- Typography: short, oversized declarative sentences for scene titles; compact explanatory copy for detail layers.
- Imagery: abstract biological and analytical language only. Existing generic science imagery can be used where it supports the story, but no people, laboratory team, product, or clinical imagery may be presented as CPL's current operational reality.

## Accessibility and Trust

- Every story scene has text content that remains meaningful without animation.
- Contrast, keyboard navigation, and link behavior remain usable across light and dark phases.
- The site respects reduced-motion settings.
- Claims describe CPL's principles, intent, and developing capability. They do not imply products, employees, research facilities, clinical evidence, regulatory status, or collaborations that are not established.

## Validation

- Automated source checks confirm the new immersive scene structure, reduced-motion behavior, and absence of source comments.
- Browser verification covers desktop and mobile layouts, normal scrolling, navigation, contact paths, and scene readability.
- Deployment verification confirms that the production site serves the committed scene version.
