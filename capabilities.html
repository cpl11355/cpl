01 / VALUE

先算清价值，
再构建证据。Value first.
Evidence next.

每一个复杂决定，都从一个更清晰的问题开始：这件事，值不值得做？Every complex decision begins with a clearer question: is it worth doing?

02 / EVIDENCE

让判断，
有来源、有边界。Ground decisions
in traceable evidence.

结论不靠气势，而靠可以复核的依据、逻辑与前提。Conviction comes from evidence, logic and explicit assumptions.
